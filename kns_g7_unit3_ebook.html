Grammar Focus

文法焦點：Where 問句與地方介系詞

P. 50

📌 句型一：Where 問句（詢問位置）

問句結構： Where + is / are + 主詞?

答句結構： It is / They are + [地方介系詞片語].

  • Where is the cat? → It is under the chair. (單數用 is / It is)
  • Where are the books? → They are on the desk. (複數用 are / They are)

📌 句型二：7 大常用地方介系詞辨析

in (在...裡面)
in the bedroom / in the box
on (在...表面上)
on the table / on the wall
under (在...正下方)
under the stairs / under the bed
behind (在...後方)
behind the door / behind the TV
in front of (在...前方)
in front of the sofa
next to / beside (在...旁邊)
next to Ron's bed
between... and... (在兩者之間)
between the books and the pen
